# Product Overview

SIMAGANG is an internship management system (Sistem Informasi Magang) for Indonesian universities. It connects students (mahasiswa), partner companies (mitra), academic advisors (dosen pembimbing), field supervisors, and department administrators.

## Core Features

- **Internship Listings (Lowongan)**: Partner companies post internship opportunities with descriptions, quotas, duration, and categories.
- **Application Flow**: Students browse and apply for internships. Partners review and approve/reject applicants. Department assigns academic advisors.
- **Guidance (Bimbingan)**: Students submit progress reports to their academic advisor (dosen pembimbing) who provides feedback.
- **Logbook**: Students record daily internship activities. Field supervisors review logbook entries.
- **Scoring (Penilaian)**: Field supervisors submit final assessments for students.
- **PDF Export**: Logbook entries can be exported to PDF.

## User Roles

| Role | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Departemen (depart) | Department admin — manages users, views students, assigns advisors |
| Mitra | Partner company — posts listings, approves applicants, manages interns |
| Dosen Pembimbing (dospem) | Academic advisor — reviews guidance submissions |
| Supervisor (spv) | Field supervisor at partner company — reviews logbooks, scores students |
| Mahasiswa (mhs) | Student — applies, submits guidance and logbook entries |

## Language

The application UI and domain terminology are in Bahasa Indonesia.
